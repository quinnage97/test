# How to use this

## Every morning

Open the page. It's at **http://localhost:4600** unless you were told otherwise —
when you start the app it prints the exact address to open, so use whatever it says. It tells you who to talk to today.

For each person you'll see **why** they've come up — their rate is ending, they
replied to you, you haven't spoken in months — and a message already written.

1. Read it. Change anything you like, it's just a text box.
2. Press the big green **Copy & open LinkedIn** button.
3. Their LinkedIn page opens. Click into the message box and paste
   (**Ctrl+V**, or **Cmd+V** on a Mac, or hold your finger down and choose Paste).
4. Press send on LinkedIn.

That's it. The list remembers what you sent and won't show you that person again
until it's time.

## The other buttons

- **They replied** — press this when someone writes back. They'll come to the top
  of your list tomorrow so you don't lose them.
- **Not now** — puts them away for three weeks.
- **Take off list** — you'll never see them again.

## When the list is empty

You're done. That's the whole idea. It gives you a short list, not everything at
once, so it's always finishable.

## The four tabs

**Today** — your list. This is the one that matters. The rest you'll barely touch.

**New people** — people you want to connect with. It writes the little note that
goes with the connection request, which roughly doubles how many accept. It only
ever gives you about twenty a day, on purpose — LinkedIn gets suspicious of more.

**Post ideas** — press the button and it writes three posts for you. Posting is
the best thing you can do on LinkedIn: one post is seen by more people than a
hundred messages, and nobody minds it. Two a week is plenty.

**Everyone** — everybody you know. Add people, or bring in your whole LinkedIn
connection list at once (there are instructions on the Import page).

## The two things worth filling in

When you open someone's page, there are two boxes that make the whole thing work:

- **Date their fixed rate ends** — this is the important one. Six months before
  that date, they'll appear on your list automatically. Every year. Forever.
  If you do nothing else, fill this in for every client you've ever had.
- **Date of their next review** — same idea, for pensions and protection.

Fill these in as you go and the list quietly turns into your best source of work.

## If a message has red writing on it

It's flagged wording that your network might not be happy with — a promise, a
"best rate", something that sounds like advice. It's only a warning. Read it,
change the message if you agree, send it if you don't. You're the adviser.

## If something looks broken

Nothing you've entered is ever lost. Close the page, open it again. If it's still
wrong, ring your son.
